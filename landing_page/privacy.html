Privacy Policy

Your reading stays yours.

FlowTrakka measures reading time without reading your documents. Here is exactly what stays on your device and what is sent when you choose to sync.

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Summary

FlowTrakka has two parts: a web app and a Chrome extension. Both keep your tracking data on your device by default. Syncing and the leaderboard are optional, and we never receive your document files or their contents.

Web app: what stays on your device

Your imported PDFs, your library, your reading sessions, your daily goal and your settings are stored in your browser on your device. You can delete a document from the app, export a backup, or clear the site's data in your browser to remove everything.

Web app: if you sign in and sync

Signing in is optional and uses your Google account. When you sign in, we store your Google account identifier, email address, display name and profile picture URL. When your device syncs, we receive for each reading session: the start and end time, the duration, the platform, the highest page reached, and the document's title and a hash of the document. We do not receive the PDF file or its text. Your display name and totals appear on the leaderboard.

Web app: anonymous usage counts

To count installs and visitors, the web app generates a random device identifier in your browser and sends it once per day when you open the app, and once when the app is installed. The request also says whether the app is running as an installed app, and the time. This identifier is not linked to your account, email or documents, and we do not store your IP address with it. If your browser's Do Not Track setting is on, the app does not send it.

Chrome extension

The extension stores tracking settings, daily focus totals, document type metadata, last-read timestamps and optional leaderboard preferences in Chrome's local extension storage. It does not store document contents, keystrokes or unrelated browsing history. Leaderboard sharing is off by default; if you opt in, it may send aggregate stats such as display name, user ID, focus totals, streak, document count and document-type totals. It does not send document titles, URLs or raw session history in those payloads.

Chrome permissions

The extension uses storage, tabs, idle and alarms to detect supported document activity, avoid counting idle time and update focus totals, plus access to the configured leaderboard backend to sync opt-in rankings. It does not execute remotely hosted code.

Email list

If you submit your email through a form on this website, for example to be told when the mobile app launches, the address is sent to the FlowTrakka backend and stored to send you product availability updates. Submitting the same address again updates the existing record.

Who processes your data

The web app is hosted on Vercel. The sync, leaderboard and email-list services run on Cloudflare. They process requests technically to deliver the service.

Your choices and deleting data

You can use the app without signing in. You can export your data, delete documents, and clear local data at any time. To ask us to delete synced account data or your email-list entry, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and its effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the FlowTrakka publisher through the contact email listed on the Chrome Web Store publisher profile.
