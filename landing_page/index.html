FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does FlowTrakka upload my PDFs?

No. Your PDFs are stored in your browser on your device. If you sign in and sync, only reading times and document titles are sent.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first load, you can open your library and read without a connection. Your time syncs later if you are signed in.

Do I need an account?

No. You can read and track time without signing in. Sign in with Google only if you want to sync your time or appear on the leaderboard.

How does it know I am reading?

It counts time while the reader is open and you are active. It pauses when you switch tabs, leave the window, or are idle for 30 seconds.

Can I bring my history from the Chrome extension?

Yes. Export a backup from the extension and import it in the web app. Importing the same file twice will not double your hours.

Is there a mobile app?

Not yet. It is coming. For now, add the web app to your home screen from your phone browser.
